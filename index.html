StudReads: university timetable maker and exam date sheet finder

StudReads turns the timetable spreadsheet your university shares into your own weekly timetable, and finds your exams in the exam date sheet. It is free, and your files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

StudReads needs JavaScript to read your files and draw your timetable.

Questions

What is StudReads?

A free planner for university students. It turns the timetable spreadsheet your university shares into your own weekly timetable, and finds your exams in the exam date sheet.

Which files can it read?

Timetables in Excel (.xlsx, .xls), OpenDocument (.ods) or CSV. Exam date sheets in PDF (including scanned ones), Excel, CSV, Word (.docx), photos and screenshots, or text you paste.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read in your browser and StudReads has no server. Your timetable and exam list are saved only in this browser.

Can I make a timetable without a file?

Yes. Click any time slot on the week grid, or “Add class”, and fill in the course, day and time.

How do I save or share my timetable?

Save it as an image, print it, add it to Google, Apple or Outlook calendar with the .ics export, keep a backup file, or send a share link that opens as an editable copy.
