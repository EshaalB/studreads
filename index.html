StudReads: free timetable generator for university students, with an exam date sheet finder

StudReads is a free timetable generator: it turns the timetable file your university shares (Excel or PDF) into your own weekly class schedule, and finds your exams in the exam date sheet. It is free, and your files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

StudReads needs JavaScript to read your files and draw your timetable.

Questions

What is StudReads?

A free planner for university students. It turns the timetable spreadsheet your university shares into your own weekly timetable, and finds your exams in the exam date sheet.

Which files can it read?

Timetables in Excel (.xlsx, .xls), OpenDocument (.ods), CSV or PDF. Exam date sheets in PDF (including scanned ones), Excel, CSV, Word (.docx), photos and screenshots, or text you paste.

Which universities does it work with?

Any university that shares its timetable or date sheet as a file. It is tested with timetables and date sheets from FAST NUCES, Punjab University (PUCIT), UMT, CUST, NUTECH, Quaid-i-Azam University, Sarhad University and the University of Connecticut.

How to make your timetable

  1. Upload the timetable file your university shared (Excel, CSV or PDF).
  2. Choose your section, then tick your courses, including any from other sections.
  3. Upload the exam date sheet to add your exams, then save, print, share or export to your calendar.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read in your browser and StudReads has no server. Your timetable and exam list are saved only in this browser.

Can I make a timetable without a file?

Yes. Click any time slot on the week grid, or “Add”, and fill in a weekly class or an exam.

How do I save or share my timetable?

Save it as an image, print it, add it to Google, Apple or Outlook calendar with the .ics export, keep a backup file, or send a share link that opens as an editable copy.
